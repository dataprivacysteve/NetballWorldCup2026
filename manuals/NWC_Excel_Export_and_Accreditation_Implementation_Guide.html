Implementation, deployment and staff operations guide for the protected Netball World Cup roster workbook and the detailed accreditation-requirement notices.

Outcome and navigation

This release gives authorised LOC staff a complete operational roster workbook and removes guesswork when a team cannot be finally accredited. It does not expose the export to SportsBB administrators, team accounts or GameDay officials.

1. What changed

Complete roster workbook

The workbook contains every recorded active player, reserve and team official for every delegation whose registration has been submitted.

Clear worksheet naming

The roster worksheet is named Full Team Roster, making its purpose clear to operational staff and NWC submission preparers.

Detailed failure reasons

When final accreditation fails, the LOC notice lists each missing roster requirement returned by the server instead of showing only a generic message.

Protected and audited

The endpoint remains LOC-only. Each successful export records the actor, timestamp, format, delegation count and person count in the LOC audit history.

No schema change is required. This release changes application logic, workbook presentation and the LOC interface only.

3

Staff export procedure

  1. Sign in to the main platform with the authorised LOC officer account.
  2. Select Settings in the Operations console navigation.
  3. Find the Netball World Cup submission panel.
  4. Select Export NWC submission.
  5. Read the personal-data warning and continue only if the destination is approved and secure.
  6. Confirm that a file named like nwc-submission-YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx downloads.
  7. Open the workbook and verify the Delegations and Full Team Roster totals before submission.
  8. Record the secure storage location and responsible staff member under the organisation’s handling procedure.
Do not download to a shared desktop, personal cloud drive, public folder or unmanaged USB device. The workbook contains names, dates of birth, contact information, biographies and operational verification statuses.

4. Workbook contents

WorksheetPurposeMain content
Read meContext and handling noticeTournament, generation timestamp, totals, authorised purpose, security and recovery notes.
DelegationsOne row per submitted delegationAssociation, delegation contacts, travel information, registration and roster statuses, accreditation date and roster count.
Full Team RosterComplete roster across included teamsActive players, reserves and all officials, with submitted role, eligibility, consent, verification and credential fields.

Full Team Roster columns

Country code, delegation, first name, middle names, last name, date of birth, nationality, category, roster type, official role, other official title, playing position or role, jersey number, captain, Head of Delegation, bench eligibility, nationality match, eligibility confirmation and reference, biography, consent status, identity-verification status, LOC person-review status and credential status.

Deliberately excluded

  • Internal database identifiers.
  • Identity-document files and object-storage locations.
  • Credential QR tokens and authentication secrets.
  • LOC review notes that are not required for the NWC submission.
Coverage rule: the export includes submitted delegations and every roster person recorded for them, regardless of whether that person is pending, verified, returned or already credentialled.

5. Accreditation guidance: no more guessing

The green approved count records individual LOC decisions. Final team accreditation also evaluates the complete roster structure. These are separate controls.

Person records complete
Individual LOC verification
Team-wide roster rules
Final accreditation

Team-wide rules checked at finalisation

  • Active-player minimum and maximum.
  • Reserve and bench limits.
  • Required official roles: team manager, coach and primary care.
  • One designated Head of Delegation or delegate.
  • Biography, nationality, player type and eligibility declarations.
  • Required photograph, guardian consent and verified identity outcome.
New behaviour: if finalisation fails, the notice displays Requirements still to resolve followed by every server-returned reason. Staff can now return the exact corrections to the delegation.

Worked example: Jamaica test record

The local Jamaica record has 12 individually verified people and satisfies its player, photograph, identity and minor-consent checks. Finalisation is still blocked because the roster has only a coach and does not contain:

  • a team manager;
  • a primary-care official; and
  • a designated Head of Delegation or delegate.

The detailed notice now states those missing requirements directly.

6. Data-protection and continuity procedure

Before export

  • Confirm an NWC submission or approved continuity purpose.
  • Confirm an encrypted destination.
  • Limit the operator to authorised LOC staff.

After export

  • Verify counts before transmitting.
  • Use only the approved NWC submission channel.
  • Delete unnecessary working copies under the retention schedule.
The workbook is not a replacement for platform backup and recovery. Continue encrypted PostgreSQL backups, object-storage backups and tested restoration exercises. Excel is an operational continuity copy and submission aid.

If an exported workbook is misplaced, shared with the wrong recipient or stored in an unapproved location, stop further distribution and follow the organisation’s data-incident escalation procedure immediately.

7. Technical implementation map

ComponentResponsibility
apps/api/src/admin/admin.controller.tsExposes GET /admin/exports/nwc-submission.xlsx under authentication and the LOC officer guard.
apps/api/src/admin/admin.service.tsLoads submitted delegations and all associated roster people, builds data-minimised status maps, and records the audit event.
apps/api/src/admin/nwc-submission-export.tsBuilds the Open XML workbook with Read me, Delegations and Full Team Roster worksheets.
apps/api/src/admin/nwc-submission-export.spec.tsVerifies active-player, reserve and team-official rows, worksheet structure, XML safety and removal of internal IDs.
apps/platform/app/lib/api.tsDownloads the protected binary response using the authenticated session.
apps/platform/app/page.tsxDisplays the Settings export panel and renders structured accreditation failure reasons.

Security controls

  • HTTP-only secure session cookie.
  • LOC officer role enforcement at the API, not only in the interface.
  • Binary download with a server-supplied filename.
  • Data minimisation at workbook construction.
  • Append-only LOC audit event: nwc_submission.exported.

9. Release acceptance checklist

  • API and platform builds complete successfully.
  • Unauthenticated export request returns HTTP 401.
  • SportsBB administrator cannot access the export endpoint.
  • LOC officer can see Settings → Netball World Cup submission.
  • Confirmation warning appears before download.
  • Downloaded file opens in Excel without repair warnings.
  • Workbook contains Read me, Delegations and Full Team Roster.
  • Active players, reserves and officials all appear.
  • Workbook contains no identity files, credential tokens or internal IDs.
  • Audit history records the successful export.
  • Failed final accreditation lists each unmet requirement.
  • Successful accreditation behaviour remains unchanged.

10. Rollback and support

This release has no migration, so rollback is application-only.

cd /var/www/clients/client0/web6/web/gameday/source
git switch --detach "$RELEASE_BEFORE"
pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
pnpm --filter api build
pnpm --filter platform build
supervisorctl restart gameday-api gameday-platform
After an emergency rollback, do not leave production detached indefinitely. Record the incident and restore the approved deployment branch under technical supervision.
