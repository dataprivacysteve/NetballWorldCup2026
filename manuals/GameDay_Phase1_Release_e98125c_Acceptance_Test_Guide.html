LOC account

Test people

Safe documents

Gate 1

Five-minute smoke test

  1. Open the Team Portal and confirm the registration/sign-in screen loads with official event branding.
  2. Sign in as a team and open Players & Officials.
  3. Edit a biography, save it and confirm a success message appears.
  4. Open the LOC roster review, select Approved/Pending/Returned and confirm each pill filters the list.
  5. Open a match team sheet and confirm eligible player dropdowns display names only.
  6. Open the privacy notice from each portal and close it using the button and keyboard.
Gate passes when: there are no blank screens, failed fetches, unhandled console errors or missing navigation; all three applications communicate with the same API/database.
Area A

Delegation registration and access

REG-01Streamlined registration fields and password confirmationTeam → LOC
Steps
  1. Open Register a delegation.
  2. Confirm Team Manager replaces Head of Delegation/Head Coach duplication.
  3. Confirm no redundant Team Contact Name or Role/Title field is shown.
  4. Enter different password and confirmation values; submit.
  5. Correct the confirmation and submit again.
Expected
Mismatch is rejected clearly. Matching values create one submitted delegation and one team login. LOC receives one pending registration.
REG-02Country selection and country identityTeam → LOC
  1. Open the country dropdown and choose Jamaica.
  2. Register using a distinct team name.
  3. Open the submitted record in LOC.
The selector shows configured countries. The database and LOC display Jamaica/JAM as the country and preserve the separately entered team name; the team name never replaces country.
REG-03Delegation approval unlocks roster—without final accreditationLOC Platform
  1. Submit a new delegation.
  2. Confirm its Team Portal says awaiting LOC approval and blocks roster entry.
  3. Approve the delegation in LOC.
  4. Refresh Team Portal.
The roster workspace unlocks. The delegation is approved for roster building but is not yet shown as finally accredited and no credentials are issued.
REG-04Registration closing date does not lock an existing teamTeam Portal
  1. Set registration close time in the past.
  2. Confirm a new delegation cannot register.
  3. Sign in to an already approved delegation.
  4. Edit an existing person or add a permitted replacement.
New team creation is closed; an authorised existing team remains able to manage its roster after the deadline.
Area B

People, profiles and sensitive documents

PPL-01Progressive entry—no minimum player lockTeam Portal
  1. Add only one player.
  2. Save the available details.
  3. Submit available records for rolling LOC review.
The one-person record saves and can be sent for early review. The 10-player minimum applies only to final team readiness, not progressive entry.
PPL-02Biography minimum and correction rightsTeam Portal
  1. Create/edit a person with fewer than 700 characters.
  2. Confirm the live character count and incomplete state.
  3. Enter more than 700 characters and save.
  4. Reopen the record and correct the biography.
Short biography does not qualify for final readiness. Long text saves without an artificial maximum, redisplays after refresh, and remains editable. The counter shows actual/700.
PPL-03Player-only nationality eligibility declarationTeam Portal
  1. Add a player whose nationality differs from the delegation.
  2. Add a broadcaster/official whose nationality differs.
Eligibility confirmation/reference appears only for the player. It does not block or appear for broadcasters, media or team officials.
PPL-04Age and guardian consent calculated from DOBTeam Portal
  1. Add an adult born in 1989.
  2. Add a person under 18 on the configured age-assessment date.
  3. Refresh and edit each profile.
Adult never shows U18 and needs no guardian consent. Minor shows U18 and requires guardian consent. Age is not hard-coded.
PPL-05Real profile photograph—not placeholder metadataTeam Portal
  1. Create a person without uploading a photo.
  2. Confirm profile completion says photo missing.
  3. Upload a real JPEG/PNG and refresh.
  4. Replace it and refresh again.
No seed/transparent placeholder counts as a photo. Genuine image is visible and marks the photo check complete; replacement displays the new image.
PPL-06Identity document upload and immediate acknowledgementTeam → LOC
  1. Select Passport or National ID, issuing country and a safe test file.
  2. Upload once.
  3. Refresh Team Portal and open the record.
  4. Open the person in LOC.
A success toast confirms receipt. Team shows Received—awaiting LOC rather than No document. LOC can see metadata and manually open the restricted file. Users are not encouraged to upload twice.
PPL-07Expiry calendar enforces tournament validityTeam Portal
  1. Select Passport and inspect the expiry calendar.
  2. Attempt a date before 26 October 2026.
  3. Select 26 October 2026 or later and upload.
  4. Select National ID and leave expiry blank.
  5. For National ID, enter an earlier date.
Passport requires a date valid through the tournament end; earlier dates cannot submit and inline guidance explains why. National ID may omit expiry, but any supplied date must also meet the tournament date.
PPL-08Edits invalidate stale person approvalTeam → LOC
  1. Have LOC approve a complete person.
  2. Confirm Approved status.
  3. Edit a material profile field or replace evidence.
  4. Refresh both portals.
The old approval is no longer treated as current. Person returns to Pending for LOC review. A biography-only non-material edit follows the configured review-preservation rule.
Area C

Rolling LOC review and accreditation

LOC-01LOC sees full submitted informationLOC Platform
  1. Open roster review for a submitted team.
  2. Inspect name, DOB, nationality, category/role, classification, primary position, bench flag, biography, photo, consent and identity metadata.
LOC can verify the actual submitted information—not only presence indicators. Identity file remains restricted to authorised manual verification.
LOC-02Approve person and remove redundant verification actionLOC Platform
  1. Complete and approve one person.
  2. Reload their row.
  3. Check Team Portal status.
Row shows LOC approved; Verify person is hidden because the decision is already complete. Team Portal shows Approved/Ready rather than a second verification request.
LOC-03Clickable Approved, Returned and Pending filtersBoth portals
  1. Prepare at least one person in each state.
  2. Click each summary pill in LOC.
  3. Repeat in Team Portal.
  4. Click the active pill again, then use Show all.
Only matching people display; active filter has a strong outline; active pill toggles back to all; Show all restores the list; empty states are explanatory.
LOC-04Status colours carry consistent meaningBoth portals

Review person cards, summary pills and category chips.

Red is reserved for incomplete/returned/error attention; yellow means pending LOC; green means approved/ready. Media/broadcast category is neutral blue, not alert red.
LOC-05Audit history identifies the delegation/countryLOC Platform
  1. Approve, return and reapprove records from two countries.
  2. Open LOC audit history.
Events show a human-meaningful target name and/or country, not only an opaque delegation UUID. Actor and timestamp remain present.
LOC-06Final accreditation remains separately gatedLOC Platform
  1. Approve only some people.
  2. Attempt Finalise team accreditation.
  3. Complete all configured team requirements and approve every person.
  4. Finalise again.
Finalisation is blocked until all requirements and individual approvals are complete. Once complete, credentials issue and the team becomes accredited.
Area D

Match team sheets

MAT-01Rolling-approved players become selectableTeam Portal
  1. Approve one complete player at person level.
  2. Do not finalise team accreditation.
  3. Open a scheduled match sheet for that delegation.
The approved player is selectable. Pending, returned, incomplete or stale-approved players are absent. Final credential issuance is not required for this selection.
MAT-02Names-only player dropdownsTeam Portal
  1. Give two eligible players jersey numbers.
  2. Open each starting-seven selector.
Options display First name + Last name only. No #1, #2 or other jersey-number prefix appears.
MAT-03No eligible players explains the causeTeam Portal
  1. Use a delegation with no current LOC-approved players.
  2. Open its match sheet.
Selectors are safely disabled and the page explains that individual LOC approval is required; it does not look like an unexplained blank dropdown.
Area E

Feedback, privacy and responsive design

UX-01Immediate success feedback prevents duplicate actionsBoth portals
  1. Save a profile, upload a photo, upload identity, submit available records and make an LOC decision.
  2. Watch after each action.
An accessible toast/snackbar confirms the specific action and fades automatically. It does not obscure the form, duplicate on one action or appear for ordinary GET requests.
UX-02Full privacy and document-handling modalBoth portals
  1. Click Data privacy notice in Team Portal and LOC Platform.
  2. Read the sections and test close button, Escape key and focus return.
A substantive notice explains purposes, data categories, sensitive-document handling, access, retention/deletion, correction rights, security, contact and complaints. Modal closes accessibly and returns focus to its trigger.
UX-03Mobile navigation remains usableTeam Portal
  1. Test at 320px, 375px and 430px widths.
  2. Scroll a long roster to the bottom.
  3. Select Home, Register, Roster, Sheets and Status.
Five equal navigation items remain visible, labels are not clipped, content is not hidden under the safe area, and the active item is clear.
UX-04Long content and approval-card spacingBoth portals
  1. Use long names, email addresses, phone numbers and a 700+ character biography.
  2. Inspect desktop, tablet and mobile.
Text wraps without overlapping adjacent fields or action buttons. Approval cards and biography content remain readable with no horizontal overflow.

Regression checklist

  • No 401/500 responses during authenticated normal use.
  • RLS still isolates each delegation and permits LOC cross-team review.
  • Uploads survive refresh and are visible only to authorised roles.
  • No real credentials, identity files or database dumps entered Git.
  • Email failures do not roll back a successful database transaction; failures are logged.
  • Existing approved teams can still sign in after deployment.
