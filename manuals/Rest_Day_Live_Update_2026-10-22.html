AreaBehavior after deployment
LOC fixture listAll matches appear by default, including earlier, final, cancelled and postponed fixtures. LOC can narrow the list using the existing checkbox.
Fixture correctionsPrepared, ready fixtures allow schedule and court changes. Cancellation is available after play starts and preserves recorded history. Upcoming fixtures can be postponed. Cancellation/postponement clears clocks, countdowns and featured broadcast selection.
