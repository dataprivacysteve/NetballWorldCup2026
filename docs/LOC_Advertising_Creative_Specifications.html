01 · Deliverables

Supply artwork at the exact canvas size

The platform never stretches advertising. It scales the image proportionately and adds transparent space when the aspect ratio does not match. Exact dimensions prevent filler, letterboxing and unexpectedly small logos.

!
One placement requires one correctly sized file. Do not submit a square logo and expect it to fill a wide banner. Build the logo, campaign message and background into the specified canvas.

03 · Production quality

Artwork that remains legible across the gym

Large screens magnify weak source images and small text. Build from original logos and photography rather than screenshots or social-media downloads.

1

Correct pixel dimensions

Export at the exact size listed. Never enlarge a small image to meet the canvas.

2

sRGB colour

Convert CMYK artwork to sRGB before export so colours remain predictable on screens.

3

Strong contrast

Use light text on dark backgrounds or dark text on light backgrounds. Avoid thin outlines.

4

Safe area

Keep logos, legal copy and calls to action at least 8% inside every edge.

5

Readable type

For the 1500×500 gym creative, use approximately 48 px or larger for essential copy.

6

Lean file size

10 MB is the enforced maximum. A clean source under 2 MB is preferred for fast venue loading.

Do

  • Use official vector or high-resolution partner logos.
  • Keep the message to one short headline.
  • Build backgrounds to the complete canvas edge.
  • Check spelling, dates, URLs and sponsor tier.
  • Preview on a dark screen before approval.

Do not

  • Submit screenshots, Word files, PDFs or SVG files.
  • Place a narrow image inside a white 1500×500 canvas.
  • Use tiny disclaimers as essential information.
  • Distort, stretch or crop partner logos.
  • Include QR codes unless tested from the gym seating distance.

04 · LOC publishing workflow

Upload, preview and approve

Advertising is managed from the LOC platform by authorized Media & Communications or LOC personnel. Every save and upload is recorded in the audit history.

Open Advertising

Sign in at platform.netballamericas.test and open the Advertising module.

Create the campaign

Enter the partner or campaign name and choose Website, In-house display, or both.

Select the placement

For website use, choose Gold, Silver, Bronze or Supporter. Upload artwork matching that exact slot.

Set rotation and destination

Choose 5–60 seconds for in-house rotation. Add an HTTPS click-through URL for website advertising when required.

Save as draft or publish

Use draft while approvals are outstanding. Activate only after brand, commercial and technical review.

Preview both surfaces

Check the public website placement and the controlled gym screen at platform.netballamericas.test/gym.
