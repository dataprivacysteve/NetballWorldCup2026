Step 2 · Test safely online

Isolated UAT environment

Do not test against the practically-live database. Give the team a separate online environment under a distinct cookie boundary, protected from search engines and public traffic.

Recommended URLs

www.uat.netballamericas.org
api.uat.netballamericas.org
teams.uat.netballamericas.org
platform.uat.netballamericas.org
stats.uat.netballamericas.org

Use COOKIE_DOMAIN=.uat.netballamericas.org. A UAT cookie must never be sent to production.

Access boundary

Protect UAT with VPN, IP allow-list or an outer identity proxy. Add noindex, nofollow, keep robots exclusion enabled, and display a permanent “UAT · SYNTHETIC DATA” banner.

Separate data plane

Use a separate PostgreSQL database and roles, separate Redis instance/namespace, and separate photo, badge and identity buckets. Never point a UAT variable at a production hostname.

Safe integrations

Send email to a capture inbox such as Mailpit/MailHog or a restricted test mailbox. Use non-public stream IDs, test sponsor destinations and fake notification recipients.
