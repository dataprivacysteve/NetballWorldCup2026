Role boundary

What the LOC account controls

The LOC account reviews and decides; it does not act as a Team Manager or SportsBB configuration administrator. Only one authorised LOC officer should use this login. Scorer, statistician, broadcaster, gate and other GameDay roles use separate role-specific access.

Security: do not share this account. If compromise is suspected, reset the password, end active sessions and notify SportsBB engineering.

Account access

Sign in securely

  1. Open the LOC Platform—not the Team Portal.
  2. Enter the authorised LOC email and password.
  3. Confirm the header identifies you as the authorised LOC officer.
  4. Sign out whenever leaving the workstation.

Use Forgot password where available or the approved administrative reset procedure. Never create shared unofficial credentials in the database.

Registration intake

Approve a delegation account

  1. Open Registrations.
  2. Check the represented country, association, team name, Team Manager, contact email and phone.
  3. Confirm the contact is authorised by the national association.
  4. Select Approve to unlock team entry, or Reject/Return with a clear reason.
This is not accreditation. Delegation approval permits the Team Manager to build the team. It does not approve people, verify identity or issue credentials.
Rolling accreditation

Review every submitted person

Detailed review: full submitted information, evidence indicators, individual decision status and final team controls.
  1. Open Team review and choose a delegation.
  2. Use Approved, Returned and Pending to filter the queue.
  3. Read the full registered name, DOB where required, nationality, category, classification/role, position, bench allocation and biography.
  4. Check the profile photograph and consent where required.
  5. For players only, check nationality eligibility when nationality differs from the team.
  6. Review identity evidence and record the decision.
Current evidence only: when a team materially edits an approved profile or replaces evidence, the old approval becomes stale and the person returns to Pending.
Restricted evidence

Manually verify identity

  • Confirm the document belongs to the named player.
  • Compare registered name, nationality and DOB with the submitted information.
  • Confirm issuing country and document type.
  • For passports, confirm expiry remains valid through the tournament end date.
  • Record Verified or Return/Reject with a specific correction note.
Restricted handling: view only when needed, do not download to personal storage, do not screenshot, print or email the document, and do not disclose it to unauthorised staff. The file is deleted after the verification decision under the platform rule.

Write concise, actionable return notes. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in notes or audit records.

Final team decision

Finalise team accreditation

Final accreditation is available only when every required team condition and current individual decision is satisfied: configured player minimum/maximum, reserve maximum, required officials, bench rules, biographies, photos, DOB/consent, identity verification and eligibility evidence.

  1. Review the approved/returned/pending counts.
  2. Resolve all pending and returned people.
  3. Confirm the full team meets configured rules.
  4. Select Finalise team accreditation.
  5. Verify the team becomes Accredited and credentials are available.
Do not bypass the gate manually. If the control remains disabled, identify the failing requirement rather than editing production records directly.
Badge production

Issue and manage credentials

Open Credentials after final accreditation. Confirm the person, role/category, delegation, access zones and credential status before printing. Revoke compromised or invalid credentials through the platform and preserve the audit trail.

Governance

Registration window and audit history

Use Settings to monitor the configured registration window and audit events. Closing registration prevents new teams from registering; existing teams retain authorised access to manage permitted changes. Audit history must identify meaningful targets such as delegation/country as well as actor and timestamp.

Configuration ownership: tournament setup, countries, official graphics, rules, sponsor content and other SportsBB-controlled configuration remain restricted to the SportsBB Administrator.
Data protection duties

Privacy and accountable review

  • Access only information needed for the active accreditation task.
  • Keep records accurate and honour correction requests through the approved workflow.
  • Do not retain identity files outside the platform.
  • Use notes for decisions—not speculation or excessive personal detail.
  • Report suspected access, disclosure or account compromise immediately.
  • Use the footer’s Data handling & privacy notice for the complete policy.
