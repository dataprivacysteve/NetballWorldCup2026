Tournament accreditation

Live status across all participating delegations. Rosters flow from delegation submission through the four-check review to issued credentials. Click any delegation to open its review queue.

Accreditation review

Every submitted person is checked against four requirements before a credential issues: a photograph on file, an identity document recorded, a date of birth (eligibility), and an assigned access category. Only a record meeting all four can be approved.

Documented exceptions. A small number of personnel need cross-category access (e.g. a team doctor needing Field of Play and Back-of-House). These are handled as committee-approved exceptions, not new categories. Reconciliation. The platform record is reconciled against the official paper accreditation process, which remains the system of record for the sanctioned event.

Issued credentials

Each approved person carries a QR credential that resolves to the verification service. Credentials are themed navy with the delegation flag and a category colour band — two distinct, fast reads. They print four to a US-Letter sheet for economical lamination. Click any pass to view it full size.

Gate check · /scan

The volunteer-facing route. A gate volunteer on a personal phone opens the camera, reads a credential's QR, and sees a clear result — granted or not valid — with the holder's photo, delegation, and permitted zones, and nothing else. Built to tolerate patchy venue wifi.
